# Gameplay

## High level

- Drifting is the core gameplay
- Player takes damage from enemies that inflict damage on non-optimal drift lines
- Player heals damage from perfect drifts
- Falling off track knocks you down levels in the tower
- Player upgrades in speed, control, and survival
- 3-5 cars with non-changing base stats
- Cosmetic upgrades
- Leaderboards
- Time pressure
- Polished effects
- Mini story (skippable/ability to turn off after first run)
- Electronic intensity building music
- Pixel art world
- Hand drawn player? (JDM art style)
