# Sub-level Design

## Gameflow

The player quickly hops between screen sized maps "floors" that present drifting and enemy challenges.

### Gameflow Questions

#### Should players be required to clear all enemies on the map?

##### Yes

- Would require all maps be circular or exiting map reloads you to front of the map

##### No

- Could lead to players ignoring combat system, but maybe the incentive is points, upgrades, etc...
  - If that were the case then players could feel the FOMO of missing enemies, but this is the roguelike mechanic of progression...
- Could enable speed run mechanic where the truly best players rely on there driving skills first
  - Would require masterful level design

##### Sometimes

- Boss fight
- Arena floor

## Thoughts

### Falling between floors

- Narrow paths / bridges
- Fragmented floors with jumps
-

### Individual map size

- Single screen size
  - User sees the entire challenge ahead of them when map loads
  - Quickly hop between Drift Tower floors
  - More punchy and action packed
- Large screen size
  - More exploratory but quick driving decisions must be made
  - Complicates level design
  - Potentially bad experience controlling a fast moving player in a fast moving map
